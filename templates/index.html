04 The History

Woven from 1995 onward.

We started our journey way back in 1995, as a supplier of hand-tufted carpets to various exporters in and around Panipat. We established ourselves as a prestigious supplier amongst our clients by producing consistently good quality products with 0% rejection, which gave us the confidence to sell our products to overseas customers directly.

As a result of constant and meticulous effort, Shandilya Home Decor gained real momentum in 2012 by signing its first direct contract with an Australia-based customer. Over the years since, we have expanded both our product range and our customer base across the globe.

05 About the Company
Complete "Floor Covering" Solutions

Rugs & carpets, completely integrated.

Shandilya Home Decor is a rugs & carpets manufacturing house, with expertise in Hand-Tufted, Hand-Woven, Hand-Knitted, Machine-Tufted and Braided constructions. We provide complete "floor covering" solutions to our customers.

Our dedicated design & merchandising team works with customers to develop custom-made programs. Every product is crafted in a sustainable environment and carries its own identity, standing up to international quality standards. Shandilya Home Decor runs a fully integrated manufacturing set-up with organized systems and work practices as per ISO 9001:2015.

11 Manufacturing

90–95% of production, in-house.

We have the required infrastructure to handle 90% to 95% of production in-house. We collect our yarns from the most reputed mills across multiple regions, and with the assistance of our production & quality team, we deliver the highest quality products to our customers.
